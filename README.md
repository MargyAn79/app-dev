# My Favorite Movie: Uptown Girls (2003)

**Uptown Girls** is a heartwarming comedy-drama directed by *Boaz Yakin*, starring *Brittany Murphy* and *Dakota Fanning*.

---

## Synopsis
Molly Gunn, the carefree daughter of a late rock legend, loses her inheritance and is forced to take a job as a nanny for Ray Schleine, a mature and hyper-organized eight-year-old girl. Despite their completely opposite personalities, they help each other grow up and heal.

## Why I Love It
* **Iconic Duo:** The chemistry and contrast between Molly's free-spirited energy and Ray's serious nature make every scene memorable.
* **Emotional Depth:** Beneath the comedy and early-2000s fashion, it tackles grief, responsibility, and inner-child healing.
* **Nostalgic Soundtrack:** Features great early-2000s tracks and Molly's dad's classic rock songs.

### Top 3 Favorite Scenes
1. **The Coney Island Teacups Scene** – Where Molly finally gets Ray to let loose and act like a kid.
2. **The Ballet Recital** – Ray performs a routine choreographed by Molly to her father's song, *"Molly Smiles."*
3. **The Pig Auction** – A classic comedic moment showing Molly's impulsive side.

> "Fundamentals are the building blocks of fun." — *Ray Schleine*
